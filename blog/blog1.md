What is Data Science to Me?

To me, data science is the gathering of data, interpreting it, and using it to achieve a goal.
For example, using geographical and population data in a city or town in order to determine 
where bus stops should be placed. The data itself would be the population in relation to a certain area,
and the goal would be to put more bus stops in densely populated areas. Modeling social topics and people is important, as it allows us to research and learn about real topics in data analysis that happen to us every day. From searching questions on our phones to accepting terms and conditions to allowing apps to track our data, all of these actions happen daily and contribute to the world of data science. A major responsibility that comes with using data about people is the ethics behind it. Are we using this data with malicious intent or are we using this data to help someone? A lot of people (especially online) extract and use our data to sell it to different companies or people, which is clearly unethical. One thing that I would love to learn throughout this course is how we can extract and use data ethically, and if there are a specific set of rules or guidelines to follow when using this data. During this course, I am hoping to better understand and learn about the variety of fields that use data science/analysis.
